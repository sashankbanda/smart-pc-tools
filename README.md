# Smart Transfer

Three simple, free tools for your Windows PC. Each one asks you one easy question at a time - like a ticket machine - and never deletes or moves anything until you have seen exactly what will happen and said yes.

| Tool | What it does for you | To open it, double-click |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Smart Transfer** | Copies (or moves) your photos, videos and files from an **iPhone**, an **Android phone**, or **any folder or drive** onto your PC - and checks that every single file arrived perfectly. | `Smart Transfer.cmd` |
| **Smart Cleanup** | Shows what fills up your PC and helps you **free up space** safely: duplicate files, leftover junk, old downloads. Also **tidies a messy folder** into neat folders. | `Smart Cleanup.cmd` |
| **Smart Memory** | Makes a **slow PC faster** by freeing up memory (RAM): closes apps you don't need and stops apps from starting by themselves. | `Smart Memory.cmd` |

Nothing is uploaded anywhere, there are no accounts and no ads. Everything stays on your PC.

## Getting started

You need **Windows 10 or Windows 11**. Nothing else to install.

1. **Download** - on this page, press the green **Code** button, then **Download ZIP**.
2. **Unzip** - right-click the downloaded file, choose **Extract All...**, and extract it to a place you will find again, for example your *Documents* folder.
3. **Open a tool** - in the extracted folder, double-click `Smart Transfer.cmd`, `Smart Cleanup.cmd` or `Smart Memory.cmd`.

> [!TIP]
> If Windows asks *"Do you want to run this file?"* or shows *"Windows protected your PC"*, that is only because the file came from the internet. Click **Run** (or **More info**, then **Run anyway**). To stop this from happening every time: before extracting, right-click the ZIP file, choose **Properties**, tick **Unblock**, and press **OK**.

**Want an icon on your desktop?** Right-click the `.cmd` file and choose **Show more options > Send to > Desktop (create shortcut)**. Smart Transfer also has an *Add a desktop shortcut* link in its window.

### How every tool works

- **One question per screen.** Click your answer - one click is enough - and the next question appears.
- **Back** is always at the bottom left, the next step always at the bottom right. The **Esc** key also goes back.
- The dark panel on the left shows **your choices so far**. Changed your mind? Click any of them to go back and change it.
- Before anything is deleted, moved or closed, you see a summary and confirm.

## Smart Transfer - copy or move your photos and files

### Before you start

| Copying from | What to do first |
| --- | --- |
| **iPhone or iPad** | Install the free **[Apple Devices](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9np83lwlpz9k)** app from the Microsoft Store - Windows needs it to see an iPhone. You do not have to open it or sign in. Then plug in the iPhone, **unlock it**, and tap **Trust** if it asks. |
| **Android phone or tablet** | Plug it in with a cable that carries data (some cables only charge), **unlock it**, swipe down from the top of the screen, tap the **USB** notification and choose **File transfer**. Samsung phones also ask *"Allow access to phone data?"* - tap **Allow**. |
| **A folder or drive** (USB stick, SD card, external disk, another folder) | Nothing - just plug it in. |

### The steps

1. **From** - pick *iPhone*, *Android* or *A folder or drive*. The app finds your phone by itself; for a folder, click *Choose a folder...*.
2. **Save to** - pick a drive or a folder. Each drive shows how much free space it has.
3. **What** - for a phone, choose **Everything new** (recommended) or *Let me choose the folders*. Files that are already on your PC are skipped automatically, so nothing is copied twice.
4. **Originals** - **Keep them** (just copy), or **Remove them** to free up space on the phone (or to *move* a folder). Nothing is removed until every copy has been checked - and you confirm first.
5. **Start** - a summary of your choices, with how long it will take. Press **Start copying**.
6. **Copy and check** - the app copies, then **checks every single file** against the original. Your PC will not go to sleep meanwhile.
7. **Done** - if you chose to remove the originals, you confirm once more, and only files that passed the check are removed.

### Good to know

- **Pause or Stop at any time.** Everything copied so far is kept. Next time, it continues where it stopped.
- **Phone unplugged or locked during copying?** The app waits. Plug it back in, unlock it, and it carries on by itself.
- **Your photos are never changed.** They are copied exactly as they are, with their original dates.
- **Files from a folder go to the Recycle Bin** when you choose to remove them, so you can still get them back.

> [!WARNING]
> Removing photos from a phone cannot be undone from the PC. **If you use iCloud Photos, removing photos on the iPhone also removes them from iCloud** and your other Apple devices. Check *Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Photos* on the iPhone first. (On Android, photos already backed up to Google Photos stay in your Google Photos.)

## Smart Cleanup - free up space, tidy up a folder

When it opens, choose what you want to do.

### Free up space

1. **Where should I look?** - choose **My files** (recommended: Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Pictures, Videos, Music, plus temporary files and the Recycle Bin), a whole drive, or any folder.
2. **Looking** - it reads everything once. Nothing is changed.
3. **What can go** - a list of suggestions. The safe ones are already ticked. Click a suggestion to tick or untick it, or **See the files** to choose file by file. Below that, a chart shows **where your space goes**.
4. **Clean up** - a last look, then it cleans up.

| Suggestion | What it means | Ticked for you? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Duplicate files | Exact copies of files you already have. One copy of each always stays. | Yes |
| Leftover junk | Downloads that never finished, and temporary files programs left behind. | Yes |
| Temporary files | Files in Windows' temporary folders that nothing needs any more. | Yes |
| Old installers | Setup files in Downloads older than a month. Your programs stay installed. | Yes |
| Empty folders | Folders with nothing in them. | Yes |
| Old downloads | Files in Downloads that have not changed for 3 months. | No - have a look first |
| Very large files | Your biggest files. | No - you choose |
| Recycle Bin | Files you deleted earlier. | No |

Removed files go to the **Recycle Bin**, so you can still put them back. Emptying the Recycle Bin (there is a button for it at the end) gives the space back for good.

**Never touched:** Windows, your programs, app settings, and files that are only stored in OneDrive's cloud.

### Organize a folder

1. Choose a messy folder - **Downloads** and **Desktop** are offered first.
2. Choose how to sort: **By type** (Pictures, Videos, Documents, Music...), **By date** (a folder per year and month), or **By type, then year**.
3. See exactly which files go where, then press **Sort**.
4. Changed your mind? Press **Undo** - every file goes back where it was. (You can also undo it later from the first screen.)

Only the loose files are sorted. Folders that are already there stay as they are, and so do shortcuts.

## Smart Memory - make a slow PC faster

When your PC has little memory (RAM) and too many apps are open, everything gets slow. Smart Memory shows how full the memory is - *Plenty free*, *Getting full* or *Nearly full* - and offers three things:

- **Close apps** - your open apps, biggest first. Tick the ones you don't need right now. They close as if you clicked their **X**, so they can still ask you to save your work. If an app stays open (probably asking you exactly that), you are told, and can force it closed if you want.
- **Startup apps** - apps that start by themselves every time you turn on the PC. Untick the ones you don't need straight away: they stay installed, and you can still open them whenever you like. **This is the lasting fix** for a slow start and a full memory.
- **Quick tidy-up** - asks every app to give back memory it is not using. Nothing is closed. It helps right before something heavy, like a game or a video call; the effect fades as apps get busy again.

**Never touched:** Windows itself, security software, and other people's apps on the same PC.

**Tip for browsers:** every open tab uses memory. Turn on **Memory Saver** - in Edge: *Settings > System and performance*; in Chrome: *Settings > Performance*.

## Questions and problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Nothing happens when I double-click a `.cmd` file | Make sure you extracted the ZIP first (**Extract All...**) - it does not work from inside the ZIP. |
| *"Windows protected your PC"* | Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. See [Getting started](#getting-started) to stop it from asking. |
| My **iPhone** does not show up | Install **Apple Devices** from the Microsoft Store, unplug and plug back in, unlock the iPhone and tap **Trust**. Tapped *Don't Trust* by mistake? On the iPhone: *Settings > General > Transfer or Reset iPhone > Reset > Reset Location & Privacy*, then plug in again. |
| My **Android** phone does not show up | Choose **File transfer** in the phone's USB notification, and try another cable - many cables only charge. |
| The phone shows up, but empty | Unlock it and keep it unlocked. An iPhone with *Optimize iPhone Storage* keeps the originals in iCloud: choose *Download and Keep Originals* in *Settings > Photos* first. |
| *"The phone has stopped sending files"* | Unplug it, plug it back in and unlock it - copying continues by itself. |
| Some files *"need attention"* after the check | Press **Try again**. Those files are never removed from the phone. |
| I freed up space but the PC shows little difference | Removed files are in the **Recycle Bin** - empty it to get the space back. On an iPhone, also empty *Photos > Albums > Recently Deleted*. |
| I want to see what happened | Every tool has an **Open the activity log** link at the bottom left. |

### Where things are kept

| What | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Your copied files | The folder you chose |
| Proof that every file was checked (reports and fingerprints) | A hidden `.iphone-transfer` folder inside the folder you chose |
| The tools' activity logs | `%LOCALAPPDATA%\SmartTransfer\logs` (paste this into File Explorer's address bar) |
| Remembered choices | `%APPDATA%\SmartTransfer` |
| The list to undo a sorted folder | A hidden `.smart-organizer` folder inside that folder |

## For advanced users

### The command-line iPhone copier

`Copy-IPhoneMedia.ps1` copies photos off an iPhone from a PowerShell window or a scheduled task, with the same checks and resume.

- **`run.cmd`** - asks you a few questions (press Enter to accept a suggestion) and copies.
- **`scan.cmd`** - only shows what is on the phone; copies nothing.

Or pass everything as arguments (find your phone's path in File Explorer under **This PC**, for example `This PC\Apple iPhone\Internal Storage`):

```powershell
# see what is on the phone, copy nothing
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File Copy-IPhoneMedia.ps1 "This PC\Apple iPhone\Internal Storage" -ScanOnly

# copy everything from 2026, keeping the month folders
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File Copy-IPhoneMedia.ps1 "This PC\Apple iPhone\Internal Storage" "D:\Photos" -Folders "2026*"

# preview first, copy nothing
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File Copy-IPhoneMedia.ps1 "This PC\Apple iPhone\Internal Storage" "D:\Photos" -Folders "2026*" -DryRun
```

| Option | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `-Source` | `This PC\Apple iPhone\Internal Storage`, a month folder inside it, or a normal folder | asked |
| `-Destination` | Folder on the PC to copy into | asked |
| `-Folders` | Which month folders, e.g. `2026*,202508_*` | all |
| `-Extensions` | File types to copy; `*` for everything | `.MOV,.JPG,.HEIC,.PNG,.MP4` |
| `-DryRun` | Show what would be copied, write nothing | off |
| `-ScanOnly` | Only show what is on the phone | off |
| `-Flat` | Put every file directly in the destination | off |
| `-Yes` | Never ask, accept the defaults | off |

Exit codes: `0` all good, `1` could not start, `2` some files failed, `3` interrupted. The app and the script share the same fingerprint list (`manifest.csv`), so each recognises what the other copied.

### Testing the tools without real files

Each tool can click through every screen by itself and save a screenshot of each one. Test runs only ever touch test folders, test apps and a test registry key - never real files, real apps or real settings.

```powershell
powershell -STA -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File app\SmartTransfer.ps1 -AutoPilot -SourceFolder C:\Test\Projects -Destination C:\Test\Out -AutoStopAfter Delete -ScreenshotDir C:\Test\shots
powershell -STA -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File app\SmartTransfer.ps1 -AutoPilot -DemoPhone C:\Test\phone -Destination C:\Test\photos -AutoInterrupt -ScreenshotDir C:\Test\shots
powershell -STA -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File app\SmartCleanup.ps1 -AutoPilot -Folder C:\Test\Mess -AutoDownloads C:\Test\Mess\Downloads -ScreenshotDir C:\Test\shots
powershell -STA -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File app\SmartCleanup.ps1 -AutoPilot -Folder C:\Test\Mess\Downloads -AutoTask Organize -ScreenshotDir C:\Test\shots
powershell -STA -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File app\SmartMemory.ps1 -AutoPilot -AutoTask Close -TestAppsFolder C:\Test\apps -ScreenshotDir C:\Test\shots
powershell -STA -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File app\SmartMemory.ps1 -AutoPilot -AutoTask Startup -TestRegistryKey Software\SmartMemoryTest -ScreenshotDir C:\Test\shots
```

(`-DemoPhone` is a folder of month folders, like `202607_a`, that plays the part of an iPhone. Smart Cleanup's test folder must be outside `AppData`, where nothing is ever suggested.)
